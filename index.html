Instructions
  1. Move from right to left with the keyboard.
  2. Jump with the up arrow or the space bar.
  3. Get all the glasses and bottles of wine.
  4. Catch the golden bread: it falls slowly, but is waiting for it worth the risk?
  5. Avoid the cats that fall and jump over the ones that run.
  6. Fill the bar up to 100 points to win. If it empties down to the skull, you lose.
