Jazz & Wine

Help Jazz catch the wine… and beware of the cats!

Move

←→ ◀▶ run left and right
↑space ▲ jump

Catch

  • +2glass
  • +5bottle
  • +10golden bread
    (rare, zigzags)

Avoid

  • -1cat
  • -10big cat
  • -10runner
    (jump over it!)

Win

Fill the life bar up to 100 points. If it empties, you lose.

Each level has a clock: reach the next level before time runs out. There is no way back.

For full screen on iPhone: tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then open Jazz & Wine from there.
