JAZZ & WINE:

this is a game where:

- A player is moving right and left (arrows or A / D) and can jump (up arrow, W or space bar).
- Has seek for collision with some class of object falling from above: Glasses of Wine and Bottles
- Has to avoid collision with an other class of object falling from above. 
- Glasses of wine sum 2 points to the score
- Bottles sum 5 points to the score
- Normal Cats substract 1 point from the score
- Ninja Cats Substract 10! points from the score
- Cats that run on the floor (jump over them!) substract 10 points, like the Ninja Cats
- When the game ends, the "Play again" button starts a new game right away.
- The golden bread is very rare (3 in the whole game), zigzags as it falls and adds 10 points

- The goal is to get to 100 points: a bar at the top shows how close you are. When you win, the dog does a somersault.
- The normal cats fall faster as the score goes up (from 70 points), and there are fewer of them at the end.
- If you get 0 points you gill get Game Over.
- Every level has a clock: if it runs out before you reach the next level, you lose.
- It also works on phones and tablets held sideways: full screen (on iPhone: Share -> Add to Home Screen) and a floating joystick in a band at the side of the game (on both sides, or only the left or the right hand, your choice): drag left or right to run, slide up to jump.
